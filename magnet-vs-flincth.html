Magnet snaps a window. Flincth sets up the whole desk.

Both are one-time purchases on the Mac App Store. Magnet moves the window you are working with, when you drag or press a shortcut. Flincth switches a whole setup: every app’s window goes to its region and the clipboard history changes with it.

Magnet: one window, right now.

Drag a window to a screen edge, press a shortcut or use the menu, and it snaps into half, a third or a quarter of the display. Then you do the next window.

⌥1 Code

Flincth: every window, one switch.

A setup names the apps for a kind of work and the region each one belongs to. Switch to it and they all move at once, and the clipboard shows only what you copied for that work.

Which one fits your day.

They solve different moments. Be honest about which moment you have more of.

Choose Magnet if

  • You arrange windows as you go, one at a time.
  • Dragging a window to an edge is the whole workflow you want.
  • You need iCloud sync or macOS 13.
  • You want the lowest price.

Choose Flincth if

  • You rebuild the same arrangement every time your work changes.
  • You want coding, writing and meetings each to have their own desk.
  • Snippets from one project keep turning up in another.
  • You would rather not grant Accessibility access.

You can also keep both. Flincth moves windows only when you switch setups and then leaves them alone, so Magnet can still snap a window in between.

Good questions.

Is Flincth a Magnet alternative?+

For people who set up the same desk again and again, yes. Flincth saves the arrangement as a setup and puts it back with one switch. If you mostly snap single windows as you go, Magnet is the closer fit.

Why does Flincth need a shortcut instead of Accessibility access?+

Flincth is sandboxed and never asks for Accessibility access. It moves windows through the “Flincth Apply” shortcut, which you install once in Apple Shortcuts. The app walks you through it.

Can either app move windows between desktops?+

Flincth cannot. It places ordinary windows on the current desktop and does not move them between Mission Control desktops or into or out of full screen.

Does Flincth snap windows when I drag them?+

No. Flincth applies your layout when you switch setups. It does not watch for drags or rearrange windows afterwards.
