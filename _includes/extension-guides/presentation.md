Presentation mode is available in Flincth Split View 1.1.0. It lets you show your audience one pane at a time while keeping your workspace ready.

## Start presenting

1. Open the workspace you want to present.
2. Focus the pane you want your audience to see first.
3. Turn on **Presentation** from the workspace toolbar.

The focused pane stays clear. Other panes show a frosted-glass cover with a small favicon and the page’s HTML title. Their sizes and positions stay as you arranged them.

## Move through your panes

Use the numbered controls to focus another pane, or click a covered pane. Previous and next controls let you step through the workspace. The cover follows your focus without reloading the pages.

Press **Esc** or use the toolbar’s exit control to leave Presentation mode. Your regular workspace returns with its layout preserved.

## What stays active

Covered websites keep running. They may still play sound, and the cover is not a security barrier for private information. Close sensitive content before sharing your screen.

An attached browser window is a real operating-system window, so it cannot receive a glass cover. Flincth minimizes inactive attached windows while presenting and restores them when you leave the mode.

Page titles and favicon addresses are used locally for these covers and are never included in usage statistics. Favicons load directly from the website’s declared icon address.
