You can have a Flincth tab in each {{ gb.name }} window, for example one workspace on your laptop screen and another on an external display.

## Open Flincth in another window

1. Go to the {{ gb.name }} window you want it in.
2. Click the Flincth Split View icon in the toolbar, then **Open Flincth Split View**, or click a workspace in the list.

Flincth uses that window's Flincth tab if it already has one, or opens one there. It never pulls the tab out of another window.

Your [keyboard shortcuts]({{ g }}/keyboard-shortcuts) work the same way: they act on the window you're in.

## What each window keeps to itself

- **Which workspace is showing.** Switch workspaces in one window and the other stays where it was.
- **What each pane is doing:** the page it's on, its Back and Forward, and whether it shows its site inside the pane or as an attached window.
- **Its attached windows.** They follow, hide and come back with their own Flincth tab only.

## What they share

- Your workspaces themselves: their names, colours, sites, shortcuts and order.
- Your settings.

When you change a workspace's layout, the latest version becomes the one the next window opens with. A window that already shows that workspace keeps its own layout.

## Closing one

Closing a Flincth tab leaves its attached windows open, in case they hold work you haven't finished. Open Flincth again in that window and it lines them up with their panes again.
