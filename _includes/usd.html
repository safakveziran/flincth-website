{%- comment -%} Prints a US dollar amount with two decimals: {% include usd.html amount="9.99" %} prints $9.99. Pass a sum to add several prices: amount is any number or numeric string, so 12 or "12.0" prints as $12.00. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _cents = include.amount | times: 100 | round -%} {%- assign _dollars = _cents | divided_by: 100 -%} {%- assign _rest = _cents | modulo: 100 | prepend: "0" | slice: -2, 2 -%} ${{ _dollars }}.{{ _rest }}
