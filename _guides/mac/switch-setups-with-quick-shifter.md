**Quick Shifter** is a circular picker for your setups. It arrived in Flincth Workspace Manager 1.2. Instead of remembering a shortcut for every setup, you press one key combination, see your setups in a circle and pick one.

## Open it

There are two ways:

- Press <kbd>⌃⌥⌘S</kbd> (Control-Option-Command-S) from any app.
- Click the Flincth icon in the menu bar and choose **Quick Shifter**.

If another app already uses <kbd>⌃⌥⌘S</kbd>, the menu shows a note that the shortcut is unavailable. The **Quick Shifter** menu item still works.

## Find the setup you want

Each segment of the circle is one setup. It shows the icons of the setup's apps side by side, with the setup's name below them. A setup with no apps yet shows a window symbol.

Move the pointer over a segment, or use the arrow keys, to highlight it. The centre of the circle repeats the highlighted setup's name and apps.

## Activate it

Click the segment, or press <kbd>Return</kbd> on the highlighted one. Quick Shifter closes and the setup activates exactly as it does from the menu bar: Flincth opens the apps that aren't running, moves each window into its region through the Flincth Apply shortcut and hides the rest.

To close Quick Shifter without switching, press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, click outside it or press <kbd>⌃⌥⌘S</kbd> again.

## More than eight setups

The circle holds up to eight setups. With more, Quick Shifter adds previous and next buttons and a page count; use them to move between pages.

## Why a setup is missing

Quick Shifter only lists setups you can use with the displays connected right now. A setup built for two displays doesn't appear while only your laptop screen is connected. Reconnect the display and the setup comes back. See [Window Layouts for Two Displays or an Ultrawide Monitor](/guides/mac/layouts-for-two-displays-and-ultrawide-monitors).

If no setup fits the current displays, Quick Shifter says so instead of showing an empty circle.

## How it looks

On macOS 26 and later, Quick Shifter uses the system's Liquid Glass material. On macOS 14 and 15 it uses a translucent background. With **Reduce transparency** turned on in **System Settings → Accessibility → Display**, the background is solid.

## Quick Shifter or a setup shortcut?

Both activate a setup the same way. A setup's own shortcut, such as <kbd>⇧⌘D</kbd>, is fastest for the two or three setups you use all day. Quick Shifter is better when you have many setups, or when you don't remember which key belongs to which. You can use both. To give a setup its own shortcut, see step 7 of [How to Create Your First Setup](/guides/mac/create-your-first-setup).
