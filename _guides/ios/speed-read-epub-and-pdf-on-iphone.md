Flincth Speed Reader reads your own books and documents one word, phrase or short sentence at a time, in one fixed spot. This guide takes a file from your iPhone or iPad to your first page.

## What it can open

- **EPUB** 2 and 3, without DRM. The book's table of contents becomes its sections.
- **PDF** with a text layer: text you could select and copy in another app.
- **DOCX** (Word). Heading styles become sections.
- **TXT**, **RTF** and **Markdown**.

It cannot open DRM-protected books, such as Kindle or Apple Books purchases, scanned PDFs without a text layer, or old `.doc` Word files. [When a document won't open](/guides/ios/when-a-document-wont-open) explains each message and what to do.

## 1. Add the file

Pick whichever is closest to where the file is:

- **From Files.** In the library, tap **Add** (the plus button) and choose **Add File…**. Pick one file, or select several at once. On an empty library, tap **Add File** in the middle of the screen.
- **From another app.** In Mail, Files, a browser's downloads or a cloud drive, tap the share button on a supported file and choose Flincth.
- **By drag and drop on iPad.** Drag a file from Files or another app onto the library.

Flincth copies the file into the app, so the document keeps opening even if you later move or delete the original. A large book can take a moment to prepare.

## 2. Open it and press play

Tap the document in the library. The reader opens at the start, or where you stopped last time, a few words back so you can pick up the thread.

Tap anywhere to start. Tap again to pause: the whole paragraph appears, with the current words marked. Tap any sentence to carry on from there.

## 3. Choose a mode and a speed

At the bottom of the reader:

- **Word**, **Phrase** or **Sentence** sets how much text appears at once. [Word, phrase or sentence](/guides/ios/word-phrase-or-sentence-mode) helps you choose.
- The **Slower** and **Faster** buttons change the speed by 25 words per minute. You can also swipe up or down while reading.

Not sure where to start? We wrote up [what speed to use in a speed reading app](/blog/what-speed-to-use-in-a-speed-reading-app).

## Moving around a book

- Tap the **Contents** button (the list icon) at the top to jump to any chapter or section.
- Swipe left to go back one sentence, right to go forward one.
- The thin bar at the bottom shows how far you are, with the time left in the section.

## Your library

Each document shows its progress. Press and hold a document, or swipe it, to **Rename**, **Reset Progress** or **Delete** it. Deleting removes it from Flincth only; the original file stays where it was.

The free plan keeps a small number of documents in the library, shown at the top of the list; Pro has no limit. Settings → Plan shows every free limit and how much of it you have used today.
