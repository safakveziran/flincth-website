There are two ways in:

- While reading, tap **Reader settings** (the gear at the top). Changes show straight away behind the sheet; **More reading settings** opens the full list.
- From the library, open **Settings** and choose **Reading & Appearance**. A preview at the top shows how text will look.

## Free for everyone

- The **Automatic**, **Light** and **Dark** themes. Automatic follows your device.
- **Text size** for the reading text, separate from the size of the rest of the app, which follows iOS text size.
- The **System** font and **OpenDyslexic**, a dyslexia-friendly font.
- **Focus guides** and **Hide controls while reading**.
- Everything iOS offers for accessibility: larger text, increased contrast, Reduce Motion and VoiceOver. Accessibility is never part of Pro.

## With Pro

- More themes (**True Black**, **Sepia**, **Paper**, **Dusk** and **Forest**) and **Custom** with your own **Background** and **Text** colours.
- More fonts, such as **New York** and **Rounded**.
- **Focus letter** on or off, and its **Focus letter color**.
- **Presets:** tap **Save Current Settings as Preset…** and give it a name, such as *Night* or *Study*. Switch between presets in one tap; swipe one away to delete it.

On the free plan, Pro settings show a lock. Tapping one opens the Pro offer and leaves your current settings exactly as they were.

## Your choices are kept

If you set something up during the [7-day Pro trial](/guides/ios/pro-trial-and-restore-purchases) and the trial ends, the reader goes back to the free look, but nothing you chose is deleted. Buy Pro later and it all comes back; Settings → Plan says how many of your saved customizations Pro would restore.
