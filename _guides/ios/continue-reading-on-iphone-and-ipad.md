With Pro (or during the Pro trial), Flincth Speed Reader keeps your iPhone and iPad in step through your own iCloud. There is no Flincth account and no sign-in.

## Turn it on

1. Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, with iCloud on.
2. Open **Settings** in the app and find **Sync**.
3. Turn on **iCloud Sync**. It is on by default for Pro and the trial when iCloud is available.

Your existing library uploads when you turn it on. The line under the switch shows the state, such as *Last synced · 2 minutes ago*.

Pro follows your Apple Account, so on the second device you only need to install the app; tap **Restore Purchases** in Settings → Plan if Pro doesn't show straight away.

## What syncs

| | Syncs |
|---|---|
| Library: titles, sections, date added | Yes |
| The document files themselves | Yes, up to 100 MB each |
| Reading position in each document | Yes |
| Reader settings and Pro customizations | Yes |
| Reading statistics | Yes, added up across devices |
| Quick Reads | No; save one to the library to sync it |

Everything goes to your private iCloud database. Flincth never sees it.

## Picking up on the other device

Open the document and it continues where you stopped, with a short note such as *Continued from iPad · Chapter 3*. If you went back on purpose on one device, the other respects that: the most recent position wins.

A document added on the other device may show **Downloading from iCloud…** for a moment; it opens as soon as the file arrives.

## If it isn't syncing

The message under the switch says what is wrong:

- **Sign in to iCloud in iOS Settings to continue reading on your other devices.** The device isn't signed in to iCloud.
- **iCloud is turned off for Flincth.** In iOS Settings, tap your name → iCloud and turn it on for Flincth.
- **Your iCloud storage is full, so new documents aren't syncing.** Free up space or upgrade your iCloud storage.
- **This document is too large to sync. It stays on the device it was added on.** Files over 100 MB stay where they were added and are marked in the library.

## Turning it off

Turning off **iCloud Sync** keeps everything on this device. If the trial ends without a purchase, sync stops the same way; nothing on your devices is deleted.
