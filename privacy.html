PRIVACY

What we collect,
and what we don’t.

Last updated 7 September 2026

Flincth is made by one small team, and this page is written to be read rather than skimmed past. It covers two separate things: the Flincth app that runs on your Mac, and this website.

The app

The Flincth app has no account system, no server, and no network access. It does not send your setups, your window layouts, your clipboard history, or any usage data anywhere, because it has nowhere to send them.

Your setups and your clipboard history are stored locally on your Mac. Deleting the app removes them along with it. Nobody at Flincth can see them, recover them, or hand them to anyone else — not because of a policy, but because they never leave your machine.

The one exception worth naming: Flincth uses the Apple Shortcuts app to place windows. What Shortcuts does is governed by Apple, not by us.

This website

The website is a set of static files. There are no accounts, no sign-up forms, no newsletter, and no comment fields, so there is nothing here for you to hand over in the first place.

A few things still happen when you visit, and you should know about each of them.

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and browser user agent in order to send you the page. This is handled under GitHub’s privacy statement, and we neither see nor keep those logs.
  • Analytics, only if you say yes. We would like to know roughly how many people visit, so the site can use Google Analytics. It loads only after you press Accept on the cookie banner. If you decline, or if you simply ignore the banner, no Google script is loaded and no request is made to Google at all.
  • Notifications, only if you ask for them. The footer carries a Notifications link. Nothing happens until you press it: only then is the OneSignal web push SDK loaded and your browser asked for permission. Once you are subscribed, OneSignal holds a browser push token and an anonymous subscription id for you, so that a notification can reach this browser. There is no name, no email address, and no way for us to tell who you are. Pressing the link again unsubscribes, and your browser’s own site settings can revoke the permission outright. See OneSignal’s privacy policy.
  • Your cookie choice. Whichever you pick is remembered in your browser’s local storage under the key flincth-analytics-consent. It stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

If you accepted and want to change your mind, use the Cookies link at the bottom of any page. Clearing your browser’s site data for flincth.com also erases the choice, and you will be asked again on your next visit.

What Google Analytics sees

Only if you accepted. It records the pages you view, roughly where in the world you are, and what kind of device and browser you use. Google shortens IP addresses before storing them, and this is not something we can switch off or configure. We use the data to count visits. We do not run advertising, we do not build profiles, and we do not sell or share this data with anyone else.

Your rights

If you are in the EU, the UK, or Türkiye, you have the right to ask what personal data is held about you, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent for analytics takes one click on the Cookies link.

Because the app holds nothing and the website holds nothing beyond what is described above, most requests are answered quickly and with very little to report. Write to us and we will tell you exactly what exists.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the date at the top changes with it. We will not quietly start collecting something new under an old date.
