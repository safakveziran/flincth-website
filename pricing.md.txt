# Pricing: Flincth

Flincth makes separate apps; each has its own price. None of them has a
subscription or an account. Prices are US App Store prices in US dollars;
Apple sets the local price in other storefronts.

- Store: {{ site.data.mac.store_url }}
- Page: {{ site.url }}/mac/workspace-manager

## Flincth Speed Reader (iPhone and iPad)

### Free

- Price: $0.00 (free download)
- Includes: every reading mode (word, phrase, short sentence) and every
  supported format (EPUB, PDF with a text layer, DOCX, TXT, RTF, Markdown,
  pasted text); light and dark themes; a dyslexia-friendly font; every
  accessibility setting
- Limits: a daily reading time, a small number of saved documents, a maximum
  speed and a daily number of Quick Reads. The exact values are set remotely
  and shown in the app under Settings → Plan.

### Pro

- Price: {% include usd.html amount=site.data.reader.pro_price %}, one-time in-app purchase
- Includes: no daily limits, higher speeds and unlimited documents; themes,
  fonts, focus letter, pause and chunk tuning; reading statistics and goals;
  iCloud sync between iPhone and iPad
- Family Sharing: included
- Subscription: none

### 7-day Pro trial

- Price: $0.00
- Started by the user in the app; asks for no payment details and never turns
  into a charge. When it ends, the app returns to the free plan and keeps the
  user's documents and settings.

- Requirements: iOS {{ site.data.reader.minimum_os | split: "." | first }} or later
- Store: {{ site.data.reader.store_url }}
- Page: {{ site.url }}/ios/speed-reader
