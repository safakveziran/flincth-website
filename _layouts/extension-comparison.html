Why does a site sometimes open as a window instead of a pane?+

Some sites refuse to be shown inside another page, and a site in a pane does not see your signed-in session. For those, Flincth opens a real browser window and keeps it lined up with the pane, so the site works exactly as it does in a normal tab.

What does Flincth ask permission for?+

Nothing when you install it. When you put a site in a pane, your browser asks for access to that one site, and only then. Every site you have allowed is listed in Flincth’s settings and can be removed there.

Does Flincth collect any data?+

No. Your workspaces are stored in your browser on your own device. Flincth has no account, no analytics and makes no network requests of its own.
